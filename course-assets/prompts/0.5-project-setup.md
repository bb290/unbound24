I want to set up a Claude project for my business, so I never have to re-explain who I am or how I work. Interview me, then write my project instructions.

How to interview me:
- Ask one question at a time. Keep questions short and plain.
- If my answer is vague, ask one follow-up. If I say "skip," move on.
- Tell me roughly how many questions are left every few questions.

Ask me about:
1. My first name and the name of my business.
2. My trade, the main services I offer, and the services I don't do.
3. My service area: the towns or areas I cover.
4. How long I've been in business, and who's on the team (just me, or how many people and what they do).
5. Who my best customers are: the kind of jobs and people I want more of.
6. The tools I use every week: scheduling, estimates, invoicing, accounting, calendar, email, texting, photos.
7. How I want you to talk to me: short and direct, or more detail and explanation.
8. My voice: ask me to paste two or three texts or emails I've already sent customers. If I don't have any handy, ask me to describe how I talk to customers in a sentence or two.
9. Anything you should never do or never say.

When we're done, write my project instructions as one block I can copy, with these sections:
- About me and my business (from my answers, in plain language).
- My customers.
- My tools.
- How to talk to me.
- My voice: three to five short notes on how I sound, based on my examples. Use my words, not marketing words.
- Standing rules:
  - Everything you write for customers is a draft. I read it and send it myself.
  - Never ask for or keep customer personal information, card numbers, or passwords.
  - If you're not sure about something in my trade, say so. I'm the expert.
  - Ask me questions one at a time.
  - My business is documented in Notion. Once it's connected, check my "Start Here" page and my SOPs before answering questions about how my business works.
  - Before writing anything customer-facing, read my "Brand" page in Notion if it exists, and match it.

Then tell me how to save it: start a new project in Claude, name it after my business, and paste the block into the project instructions. Remind me to work inside this project from now on.

Start with question 1.
