How to interview me:
- Ask one question at a time.
- Start by asking me to list every app, program, or service I use or pay for, even the small ones. Prompt me with categories if I get stuck: phone and texting, email, calendar, scheduling and dispatch, estimates and quotes, invoicing and payments, accounting, CRM or customer list, photos and files, website, Google Business Profile, reviews, social media, marketing, payroll, and anything my crew uses.
- Then, for each tool, ask: what step of my business it runs, who uses it, about what it costs each month, and where the login is kept (for example "password manager" or "my phone"). Never ask for or write down a password.

Write it up as a table with these columns: Tool, What it does for us, Step (Attract, Convert, Deliver, Retain, or Back office), Who uses it, Cost per month, Where the login lives.

Under the table, add:
- Total monthly cost.
- Overlaps: any two tools that seem to do the same job.
- Gaps: any step of my business that doesn't have a tool, or that lives only in my head or on paper.
- Unused: anything I pay for but barely use.

Don't tell me to buy anything new. Just help me see what I have.
